How to Play / Rules

Basic Rules

  • Aim for the "Target" number displayed at the top.
  • Tap moving animals (numbers) to add to your Sum.
  • Match the exact Target number for a SUCCESS! Clear the game with 3 successes.
  • Exceeding the Target results in a FAIL, resetting your current Sum.
  • Animals escaping through wall gaps will trigger new numbers to spawn.

Scoring System

  • Time Bonus: Clear faster to score higher!
  • Fail Penalty: Points are deducted for every Fail (exceeding the Target).
  • Escape Penalty: Points are deducted when animals escape through the gaps.
  • Difficulty Bonus: Higher difficulty levels yield higher score multipliers.
